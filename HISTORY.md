# Proto-Minitongue History

This file records the language's in-world diachrony, not repository-development history.

Use it for reconstructed stages, ordered sound changes, analogy, reanalysis, grammaticalization, semantic change, lexical replacement, contact effects, and derivations needed to explain contemporary forms.

## Stages

Proto-Minitongue has one reconstructable ancestral stage, **Pre-Proto-Minitongue**, immediately preceding canonical Proto-Minitongue. Additional earlier stages are not currently posited.

## Changes

The historical phonology has the following canonical architecture; exact segment-by-segment outputs and chronology remain UNSPECIFIED.

- Proto-Minitongue's simple `(C)V(C)` phonotactics are partly the result of historical reduction or repair of earlier consonant clusters and vowel hiatus rather than being wholly inherited unchanged.
- Assimilation and fusion at morpheme boundaries are a major consonantal historical process.
- Vowel contact has mixed, sequence-specific historical outcomes rather than one universal repair.
- Regular sound change applies substantially across morpheme boundaries. Historical affix–stem sequences may therefore yield synchronically predictable fusion and allomorphy while remaining morphologically analyzable.
